# Cheeseburger Bowls
Status: Stage 1 - Draft / testing
Category: Beef

## Planning Summary
- Servings: 4
- Estimated prep time: 20 minutes
- Estimated cook time: 25 minutes
- Protein: Ground beef
- Cuisine or flavor direction: Cheeseburger-inspired bowls

## Equipment
- Rimmed sheet pan
- Large skillet
- Small mixing bowl

## Basic Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 425 F. Toss the quartered potatoes with 1 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Roast for 22 to 28 minutes, turning once, until browned and fork-tender.
2. Whisk 1/3 cup mayonnaise, 2 tbsp ketchup, 1 tbsp yellow mustard, and 1 tbsp pickle brine in a small bowl. Refrigerate until serving.
3. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1 lb ground beef and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking it into bite-size crumbles, until no pink remains. Drain excess fat if needed.
4. Stir 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, the remaining 1 tsp kosher salt, and remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper into the beef. Cook for 1 minute and remove from the heat.
5. Divide the lettuce, roasted potatoes, seasoned beef, tomatoes, cheddar, pickles, and red onion among 4 bowls. Drizzle with burger sauce and serve.
