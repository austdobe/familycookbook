# Philly Cheesesteak Bowls
Status: Stage 1 - Draft / testing
Category: Beef

## Planning Summary
- Servings: 4
- Estimated prep time: 20 minutes
- Estimated cook time: 25 minutes
- Protein: Thinly sliced beef
- Cuisine or flavor direction: Philly cheesesteak-inspired

## Equipment
- Medium saucepan with lid
- Large cast-iron skillet
- Cutting board

## Ingredients
| Quantity | Ingredient | Preferred version/type | Acceptable alternatives | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 1/2 cups | long-grain white rice | rinsed | brown rice | Bowl base |
| 3 cups | low-sodium beef broth |  | water | For rice |
| 1 1/4 lb | sirloin steak | very thinly sliced | shaved beef | Slice across grain |
| 1 1/2 tsp | kosher salt | divided | fine sea salt |  |
| 1/2 tsp | black pepper | divided |  |  |
| 2 tbsp | avocado oil | divided | olive oil |  |
| 1 | yellow onion | thinly sliced | sweet onion |  |
| 2 | bell peppers | thinly sliced | any color |  |
| 8 oz | cremini mushrooms | sliced | white mushrooms |  |
| 2 cloves | garlic | minced | 1/2 tsp garlic powder |  |
| 1 tbsp | Worcestershire sauce |  | soy sauce |  |
| 6 oz | provolone cheese | sliced or shredded | white American cheese |  |

## Basic Instructions
1. Combine 1 1/2 cups rinsed rice and 3 cups beef broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from the heat and rest covered for 5 minutes before fluffing.
2. Pat the sliced steak dry and season it with 3/4 tsp kosher salt and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet over high heat.
3. Cook the steak in a single layer, working in two batches if necessary, for 1 to 2 minutes per side until browned. Transfer it to a plate before it overcooks.
4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp avocado oil, onion, bell peppers, mushrooms, remaining 3/4 tsp kosher salt, and remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper. Cook over medium-high heat for 8 to 10 minutes until browned and tender.
5. Stir in 2 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce for 30 seconds. Return the steak and its juices to the skillet and toss until hot.
6. Divide the rice among 4 bowls. Top with the steak and vegetables, add the provolone, and cover each bowl briefly so the cheese softens before serving.

## Notes
- Stage 1 draft; freeze the steak for 20 minutes before slicing to make thin cuts easier.
