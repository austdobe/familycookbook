# Burgers and Fries
Status: Stage 1 - Draft / testing
Category: Beef

## Planning Summary
- Servings: 4
- Estimated prep time: 20 minutes
- Estimated cook time: 35 minutes
- Protein: Ground beef
- Cuisine or flavor direction: Classic American

## Equipment
- 2 rimmed sheet pans
- Large mixing bowl
- Cast-iron skillet or griddle
- Instant-read thermometer

## Ingredients
| Quantity | Ingredient | Preferred version/type | Acceptable alternatives | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 lb | russet potatoes | scrubbed | Yukon Gold potatoes | Cut into 1/2-inch fries |
| 2 tbsp | olive oil | extra-virgin | avocado oil | For fries |
| 2 tsp | kosher salt | divided | fine sea salt | For fries and burgers |
| 1 tsp | garlic powder |  | onion powder | For fries |
| 1/2 tsp | smoked paprika |  | sweet paprika | For fries |
| 1 1/2 lb | ground beef | 80/20 | 85/15 ground beef | Form into 4 patties |
| 1 tsp | black pepper | freshly ground |  | For burgers |
| 4 slices | cheddar cheese | sharp | American cheese | Optional |
| 4 | hamburger buns | brioche or potato | whole-wheat buns | Split |
| 4 leaves | lettuce | crisp | shredded lettuce | For serving |
| 1 | tomato | ripe |  | Sliced |
| 1/2 | red onion | small | sweet onion | Thinly sliced |
| 1/4 cup | burger sauce | favorite prepared sauce | ketchup and mustard | For serving |

## Basic Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 450 F. Place one empty sheet pan in the oven while it heats. Toss the 1/2-inch potato fries with 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp garlic powder, and 1/2 tsp smoked paprika.
2. Carefully spread the fries on the hot pan in one layer. Bake for 20 minutes, flip, then bake 10 to 15 minutes more until deeply golden with crisp edges.
3. Divide 1 1/2 lb ground beef into 4 loose portions and shape patties slightly wider than the buns. Season both sides with the remaining 1 tsp kosher salt and 1 tsp black pepper.
4. Heat a cast-iron skillet or griddle over medium-high heat. Cook the patties for 3 to 5 minutes per side, until browned and the centers reach 160 F. Add 1 cheese slice to each patty during the final minute, if using.
5. Toast the 4 split buns cut-side down for 1 to 2 minutes. Build each burger with sauce, lettuce, tomato, onion, and a cooked patty. Serve immediately with the hot fries.

## Notes
- Stage 1 draft; adjust seasoning and fry timing after the first family cook.
