# Bavarian Hunter's Pork Chops with Creamy Mushroom Gravy
Status: Stage 1 - Draft / testing
Category: Pork

## Planning Summary
- Servings: 4
- Estimated prep time: 20 minutes
- Estimated cook time: 35 minutes
- Protein: Pork chops
- Cuisine or flavor direction: Bavarian-inspired mushroom gravy

## Equipment
- Large skillet with lid
- Plate
- Whisk
- Instant-read thermometer

## Basic Instructions
1. Pat the 4 pork chops dry. Season both sides with 1 tsp kosher salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper, and 1 tsp paprika, then dust with 1 tbsp flour.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the pork chops for 3 to 4 minutes per side until browned. Transfer them to a plate; they will finish cooking in the gravy.
3. Reduce the heat to medium and add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, 12 oz mushrooms, sliced onion, remaining 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and remaining 1/4 tsp black pepper. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes until the mushrooms release their liquid and begin to brown.
4. Stir in 2 minced garlic cloves and the remaining 1 tbsp flour for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in 1 cup chicken broth, scraping the browned bits from the skillet.
5. Whisk in 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, and 1/2 tsp thyme. Simmer for 3 minutes, then stir in 1/2 cup heavy cream.
6. Return the pork chops and their juices to the skillet. Cover and simmer gently for 5 to 8 minutes, until the gravy coats a spoon and the pork reaches 145 F.
7. Rest the pork in the gravy for 3 minutes. Garnish with 1 tbsp chopped parsley and serve with potatoes, noodles, or a vegetable side.

## Notes
- Stage 1 draft; do not boil the cream sauce or the pork may become tough.
