# Dry Rub Grilled Chicken
Status: Stage 1 - Draft / testing
Category: Chicken

## Planning Summary
- Servings: 4-6
- Estimated prep time: 15 minutes
- Estimated cook time: 35 minutes
- Protein: Chicken thighs
- Cuisine or flavor direction: Smoky savory grill rub

## Equipment
- Grill
- Small mixing bowl
- Tongs
- Instant-read thermometer

## Basic Instructions
1. Pat 2 1/2 lb chicken thighs dry. Mix 2 tsp kosher salt, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp black pepper, 1/2 tsp dried thyme, and 1/4 tsp cayenne in a small bowl.
2. Rub the chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil, then coat all sides with the dry rub. Refrigerate uncovered for 30 minutes when time allows, or grill immediately.
3. Heat the grill for two-zone cooking, with one side at medium-high heat and the other side low or unlit. Clean and oil the grates.
4. Place the chicken skin-side down over indirect heat. Cover and grill for 20 minutes, then turn and cook 10 to 15 minutes more.
5. Move the chicken briefly over direct heat, turning as needed, until the skin is crisp and browned. Remove each piece when its thickest part reaches 175 F without touching bone.
6. Rest the chicken for 5 to 10 minutes before serving or attaching it to a composed meal with separate side recipes.

## Notes
