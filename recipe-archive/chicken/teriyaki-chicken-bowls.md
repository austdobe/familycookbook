# Teriyaki Chicken Bowls
Status: Stage 1 - Draft / testing
Category: Chicken

## Planning Summary
- Servings: 4
- Estimated prep time: 20 minutes
- Estimated cook time: 25 minutes
- Protein: Chicken thighs
- Cuisine or flavor direction: Japanese-American teriyaki

## Equipment
- Medium saucepan with lid
- Large skillet
- Small mixing bowl

## Basic Instructions
1. Combine 1 1/2 cups rinsed jasmine rice and 2 1/4 cups water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook for 15 minutes. Remove from the heat and rest covered for 5 minutes.
2. Whisk 1/3 cup soy sauce, 1/4 cup water, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp grated ginger, 2 minced garlic cloves, and 1 tbsp arrowroot powder until no lumps remain.
3. Heat 1 tbsp avocado oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season the chicken with 1/2 tsp kosher salt and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned and it reaches 165 F.
4. Add 4 cups broccoli and 2 tbsp water to the skillet. Cover for 3 minutes, then uncover and cook until the broccoli is bright green and crisp-tender.
5. Whisk the teriyaki sauce again, pour it into the skillet, and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until glossy and thick enough to coat the chicken. Stir in 1 tsp toasted sesame oil.
6. Divide the rice among 4 bowls. Top with teriyaki chicken and broccoli, then finish with green onions and sesame seeds.

## Notes
- Stage 1 draft; reduce brown sugar to 2 tbsp if the family prefers a less-sweet sauce.
